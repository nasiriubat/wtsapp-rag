Re-index rebuilds a group's chunks from its messages in the background; answers keep working meanwhile. Use it after changing how chunks are built.

Import chat history

The assistant only sees messages sent after it joined. To answer about anything older, export the chat on the phone with ⋮ → More → Export chat → Without media and upload the .txt here. Re-uploading the same file is safe; duplicates are skipped. Answers drawn from imported messages carry a text citation instead of a quote-reply, because the channel cannot quote a message it never saw.

Erase a member

Deletes everything they wrote, their questions and their corrections, and rebuilds the chunks that contained them. To keep them erased as they keep writing, add them to the group's opt-out list as well. Sender ids appear in the group's exported messages.
