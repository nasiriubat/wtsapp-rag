Import chat history

The assistant only sees messages sent after it joined. To answer about anything older, export the chat on the phone with ⋮ → More → Export chat → Without media and upload the .txt here. Re-uploading the same file is safe; duplicates are skipped. Answers drawn from imported messages carry a text citation instead of a quote-reply, because the channel cannot quote a message it never saw.

Erase a member

Deletes everything they wrote and rebuilds the chunks that contained it. To keep them erased as they keep writing, add them to the group's opt-out list as well.
